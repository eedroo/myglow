# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data MYGLOW processes, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have over it.

## 1. Data controller

The controller of your data is **Onda**, Av. Miguel Bombarda, 110, Lisbon, Portugal, tax number (NIF) 910888961.

For any privacy question, write to [privacidade@onda.work](mailto:privacidade@onda.work).

## 2. Data we collect

- **Account:** name, email, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), language, theme, time zone, and the dates you signed up and accepted these documents.
- **Birth data:** date, time (if you know it) and place of birth, and the natal chart calculated from them.
- **Journal:** the texts you write (intentions, banishings, gratitude, reflections, summaries, notes), ritual and body-care checks and, with your explicit consent, **wellbeing data**: mood, how you woke up, sleep and weight.
- **Planners:** intentions, goals, titles and reflections for the week, month and year.
- **Grimoire:** completed lessons, reviews, quiz results and badges.
- **Glow:** points, levels and streaks.
- **Preferences:** reminder times and choices, hemisphere, pronouns and sleep goal.
- **Notifications:** push subscriptions for your devices (the push service's technical address and encryption keys) and the history of reminders sent.
- **Minimal technical data:** IP address and browser information, used only for security and to limit abuse (for example, too many login attempts).

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- **Providing the service** (account, journal, planners, Grimoire, readings, reminders, export): performance of the contract you accept when you create your account.
- **Wellbeing data** (mood, sleep, weight): **explicit consent**, used only in your own statistics and journal. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings without affecting the rest of the app.
- **Security and abuse prevention** (attempt limits, technical logs): legitimate interest in protecting your account and the service.
- **Service emails** (email confirmation, password recovery, security notices): performance of the contract. We do not send marketing.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

Personalised readings (day, week, month and rituals) are generated by an AI model from an external provider. For this we send **only**:

- astrological facts we calculate (planet positions, Moon phases, aspects);
- a summary of your natal chart (signs and positions, without your birth date, time or place);
- the app language and the pronouns you chose.

We **never** send your name, email, place or date of birth, or anything from your journal or planners — including intentions, goals, mood, sleep and weight.

If you don't want personalised readings, you can leave your birth data out or delete your account; the rest of the journal works without AI.

## 5. Processors

We use these providers, which process data only on our behalf and following our instructions:

- **Vercel** — app hosting;
- **Neon** — database;
- **Google (Gemini API)** — AI-generated readings;
- **Inngest** — scheduled tasks (readings and reminders);
- **Upstash** — usage limits (security);
- **Resend** — sending emails.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, namely the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## 6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, **all your data is removed immediately** from the database. It may remain in encrypted backups for up to {{DIAS_BACKUP}} days, after which it is erased.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** and **portability** — in Settings › Privacy, “Export my data” downloads everything as a JSON file;
- **rectification** — you can edit your data in the app at any time;
- **erasure** — in Settings › Danger zone, “Delete account” removes everything;
- **withdraw consent** for wellbeing data in Settings, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing;
- **object** to and **restrict** processing, where the law provides;
- lodge a **complaint** with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) or the supervisory authority in your country.

To exercise any of these rights, write to [privacidade@onda.work](mailto:privacidade@onda.work).

## 8. Minimum age

MYGLOW is for people aged **16 or over**. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn that we have, we delete the account.

## 9. Cookies

We only use **essential** cookies: session (to keep you signed in), language and app preferences (for example, reading the Grimoire in Portuguese or hiding a notice). We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the version at the top and ask you to accept it again in the app before you continue.
